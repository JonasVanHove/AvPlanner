# Buddy Battle - Quick Start Guide

## 5-Minute Setup

1. **Access Gamification**
   - Go to your team page
   - Click the golden "Buddy" button (top right area)
   - Password, you need to know :)

2. **Create Your Buddy**
   - Click "Create New Buddy"
   - Choose a name (e.g., "Flame Dragon", "Water Spirit")
   - Pick your element type (🔥 Fire, 💧 Water, 🌍 Earth, 💨 Air, ⚡ Electric)
   - Choose colors (primary, secondary, accent)
   - Click "Create"

3. **Your First Battle**
   - Navigate to "Battle Arena"
   - Select "Training Battle" (practice vs AI)
   - Click opponent to fight
   - Follow on-screen battle prompts

4. **Earn Points**
   - Mark your availability daily (10 points)
   - Complete training battles (20 points per win)
   - Do daily quests (50-100 points)

5. **Upgrade Your Buddy**
   - Accumulate upgrade points (1 per week)
   - Increase stats: HP, Attack, Defense, Speed, etc.
   - Buy items from shop with buddy points

## Quick Reference

### Battle Controls
| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Attack | Click "Attack" button |
| Special Move | Click ability (if ready) |
| Use Item | Click potion icon |
| Defend | Click shield button |
| Pass | Click "Pass" to skip turn |

### Stat Glossary
- **HP**: Health points (reach 0 = lose battle)
- **Attack**: Physical damage dealing
- **Defense**: Reduce incoming damage
- **Speed**: Who goes first (higher wins)
- **Sp. Attack**: Special move damage
- **Sp. Defense**: Reduce special damage
- **Crit**: Chance for 1.5x damage

### Daily Routine (5 minutes)
1. ✅ Mark availability (earn 10 points)
2. ⚔️ Fight 1-2 training battles (earn 40 points)
3. 📦 Collect daily quest rewards (50-100 points)
4. 📈 Upgrade 1 stat or buy 1 item
5. 🏆 Check leaderboards

### Type Advantages
```
🔥 Fire → 🌍 Earth    (+20% damage)
🌍 Earth → 💨 Air     (+20% damage)
💨 Air → 💧 Water     (+20% damage)
💧 Water → 🔥 Fire    (+20% damage)
⚡ Electric → Water  (+20% damage)
```

## Beginner Tips

✨ **Tip 1**: Always mark your availability first - it funds all upgrades!

✨ **Tip 2**: Start with high HP build - more survivability while learning

✨ **Tip 3**: Pick same type buddies initially - learn matchups gradually

✨ **Tip 4**: Complete all tutorials - they grant bonus items

✨ **Tip 5**: Join team battles for extra rewards and fun!

✨ **Tip 6**: Check daily quests - easy points if you focus on them

✨ **Tip 7**: Save points for defensive stats early, attack late game

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake 1**: Ignoring type advantages (20% damage difference!)

❌ **Mistake 2**: Spending all points on cosmetics early (you need stat upgrades!)

❌ **Mistake 3**: Never defending (takes half damage - good strategy!)

❌ **Mistake 4**: Forgetting daily quests (easiest points source!)

❌ **Mistake 5**: Battling immediately at level 1 (train first, then PvP!)

❌ **Mistake 6**: Ignoring team events (major bonus participation!)

## Progression Milestones

```
Level 1-5:    Tutorial → First training battle win

Level 15-30:  Beat training & PvP battles → First item purchase
```

## Battle Quick Tips

**Before Battle**:
- Make sure HP > 0
- Check item inventory for potions
- Look at opponent's stats

**During Battle**:
- Speed stat goes first (advantage!)
- Use type advantage if available (+20%)
- Defend when at <30% HP
- Use special moves on cooldown when ready

**After Battle**:
- Claim rewards (XP + points)
- Check new unlocks
- Review battle summary
